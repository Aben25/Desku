1Desku — a cute desk agent living in an old Android phone · 3 directions × 6 states
The trend we're riding. Personal agents are arriving as named, cute characters you talk to like a colleague, that keep notes and come back to you. Desku is ours: an original blue blob with a grandpa look — round glasses, bushy white brows, a little mustache — no face borrowed from anyone else. Expressions carry state (sleepy, attentive, thinking, talking, shushed) instead of abstract spinners.
Honesty & privacy. Wake phrase "Hey Desku" is labelled coming soon everywhere; the Talk button is the primary action. Only things you ask Desku to remember are saved; every memory has Forget. Mic and camera state is always visible, and a muted Desku visibly closes its eyes and mouth.
Assumptions. Portrait phone on a desk stand, read at arm's length: body copy 18–24px, tap targets ≥ 64px. Kiosk mode hides Android bars. Mode left open: 1a and 1c are dark, 1b is light to compare. Figtree + DM Mono, sky blue #4a9be8.
Demo flow covered. Idle → Listening ("Help me plan my afternoon") → Speaking → Camera read of a handwritten list → pick one priority → remembered + 45-min check-in → Memories & privacy. Each card ends with Desku's expression sheet.
